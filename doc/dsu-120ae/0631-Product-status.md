# Product status

Source: <https://developer.arm.com/documentation/107721/0001/Product-and-document-information/Product-status>

### Product status

All products and Services provided by Arm require deliverables to be prepared and made available at different levels of completeness. The information in this document indicates the appropriate level of completeness for the associated deliverables.

### Product completeness status

The information in this document is Final, that is for a developed product.

### Product revision status

This product is r0p1, which indicates the revision status of the product described in this manual, where:

r `(value)`
:   Identifies the major revision of the product, for example, r1.

p `(value)`
:   Identifies the minor revision or modification status of the product, for example, p2.
