##### `0x81C0`, L1I\_CACHE\_HIT\_RD, Level 1 instruction cache demand fetch hit

The counter counts each demand fetch counted by [L1I\_CACHE\_RD](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x8141--L1I-CACHE-RD--Level-1-instruction-cache-demand-fetch?lang=en#event_l1i_cache_rd) that hits in the Level 1 instruction or unified cache.
