# ​A1.7.3 General taxonomy of errors

Source: <https://developer.arm.com/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors>

##### A1.7.3 General taxonomy of errors

###### A1.7.3.1 Error detection

R FHXWP
:   When a component accesses memory or other state, an [error](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-2-Faults--errors--and-failures?lang=en#mdterm_ras_error) might be detected in that memory or state.

I WKPVR
:   The error might be [corrected](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/Glossary?lang=en#glossdef_corrected_error) or [deferred](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/Glossary?lang=en#glossdef_deferred_error) by the component, or [signaled](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_signal) to another component as either a [Deferred error](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/Glossary?lang=en#glossdef_deferred_error) or a [Detected error](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/Glossary?lang=en#glossdef_detected_error).

###### A1.7.3.2 Error propagation

R LRZDN
:   A transaction occurs when a producer of the transaction passes a value or other signal to a consumer of the transaction.

I VYCCX
:   Transactions are part of the service provided by the producer for the consumer.

I RHGDL
:   In many protocols and service interface definitions, a high-level transaction consists of a sequence of operations, for instance between a *Requester* and a *Completer*.

    For the purposes of this manual, the most basic form of a unidirectional transfer between a *producer* and *consumer* is considered as a [transaction](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_transaction).

    That is, each one of the sequence of operations is considered a separate [transaction](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_transaction). For some operations, such as a request, the Requester is producer and the Completer is the consumer. For other operations, such as a response, the Completer is producer and the Requester is the consumer.

R SKZZG
:   An error is propagated by the producer of a [transaction](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_transaction) when the service interface is incorrect because of the error. The error is propagated to the consumer.

R CHCCV
:   An error is [propagated](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_propagate) by deviations from [correct service](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-2-Faults--errors--and-failures?lang=en#mdterm_correct_service), including when any of the following occurs that would not have been permitted to occur had the fault not been activated:

    - A [corrupt](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-2-Faults--errors--and-failures?lang=en#mdterm_ras_corrupt) value is passed from producer to consumer.
    - A transaction or other operation occurs that should not have occurred.
    - A transaction or other operation that should have occurred does not occur.
    - A loss of uniprocessor semantics or any other loss of coherency in a multiprocessor coherent system is observed.
    - Changing the timing and/or order of transactions or other operations such that the timing and/or order of those transactions or operations is incorrect. In this case, the service interface defines acceptable timings and/or orders for transactions and other operations.

R YFBRV
:   The service interface for a [transaction](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_transaction) might include means to signal that the transaction is propagating either of the following:

    - A [Detected error](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/Glossary?lang=en#glossdef_detected_error).
    - A [Deferred error](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/Glossary?lang=en#glossdef_deferred_error).

R BHWVX
:   An error is silently propagated by the producer of a transaction if the consumer of the transaction cannot detect the error and consumes an [undetected error](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-2-Faults--errors--and-failures?lang=en#mdterm_ras_undetected_error) because of the transaction. This might be because of one of the following:

    - The error is present on the transaction, but was not detected by the producer. The error is [silently propagated](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_silently_propagate) by the producer.
    - The error is present on the transaction, but was not [signaled](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_signal) to the consumer as an error. For example, a [corrupt](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-2-Faults--errors--and-failures?lang=en#mdterm_ras_corrupt) value was passed in the transaction with no indication that it was corrupt. The error is [silently propagated](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_silently_propagate) by the producer.

R FPBYS
:   A latent, possibly detectable, error is [silently propagated](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_silently_propagate) by the consumer of an otherwise correct transaction if the transaction causes the error to become [undetectable](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-2-Faults--errors--and-failures?lang=en#mdterm_ras_undetected_error).

    > Example A1-2
    >
    > A partial write to a [protection granule](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/Glossary?lang=en#glossdef_protection_granule) removes [poison](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_poisoned), leaving the unchanged portion of the location [corrupt](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-2-Faults--errors--and-failures?lang=en#mdterm_ras_corrupt). To implement a partial write, the consumer logically reads the current value of the location, modifies the value, and then writes the modified value back. These are internal transactions in the consumer that silently propagate the error. In this example there was no error at the producer nor on the transaction.

I QXRLB
:   Errors might be [propagated](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_propagate) by components in a system until one of the following occurs:

    - They are masked and do not affect the outcome of the system.

      The error might be masked because a corrupt value is discarded or overwritten, or the error is detected and removed.
    - They affect the service interface of the system and possibly cause failure. If the error has been [silently propagated](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_silently_propagate) to the service interface, then:

      - This is a Silent Data Corruption, SDC.
      - The rate of such failures, measured as the number of failures per billion device-hours of operation, is called the Silent Data Corruption Failure-in-Time rate, [SDC](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_sdc) FIT rate.

      Alternatively, the error might have been detected, causing the system to invoke error handling and recovery. For more information, see *Arm® Reliability, Availability, and Serviceability (RAS) System Architecture, for A-profile architecture* (ARM IHI 0100).

###### A1.7.3.3 Infected and poisoned

R KNHWB
:   The state of a component becomes infected when the component consumes an [Uncorrected error](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/Glossary?lang=en#glossdef_uncorrected_error) that updates the state.

R TZBSW
:   A value is poisoned in the state of a component if it is marked as being in error, such that a subsequent access of the state will detect the value is so marked and is treated as a [Detected error](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/Glossary?lang=en#glossdef_detected_error).

I YBMFK
:   [Poison](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_poisoned) is used to [defer](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/Glossary?lang=en#glossdef_deferred_error) an error.

###### A1.7.3.4 Containable and uncontainable

R DXQRD
:   An undetected error is uncontained at the component that failed to detect it.

R RJYRQ
:   A [silently propagated](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_silently_propagate) error is [uncontained](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_uncontained) at the component that [silently propagated](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_silently_propagate) it.

R GJQNR
:   A [Detected Uncorrected error](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/Glossary?lang=en#glossdef_detected_uncorrected_error) is uncontainable at the component if it might be [uncontained](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_uncontained) at the component. A [Detected Uncorrected error](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/Glossary?lang=en#glossdef_detected_uncorrected_error) is containable at the component if it is not [uncontainable at the component](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_uncontainable_at_component). If the component cannot determine whether a [Detected Uncorrected error](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/Glossary?lang=en#glossdef_detected_uncorrected_error) is [uncontainable at the component](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_uncontainable_at_component) or [containable at the component](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_containable_at_component), then the component treats the [Detected Uncorrected error](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/Glossary?lang=en#glossdef_detected_uncorrected_error) as [uncontainable at the component](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_uncontainable_at_component).

I MRDMR
:   An error that is [uncontainable at the component](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-A-Arm-Architecture-Introduction-and-Overview/-Chapter-A1-Introduction-to-the-Arm-Architecture/-A1-7-Reliability--Availability--and-Serviceability/-A1-7-3-General-taxonomy-of-errors?lang=en#mdterm_ras_uncontainable_at_component) might be containable at the system level.

I NWZGB
:   Reporting an error as containable allows software to contain the error. This does not mean that hardware has contained the error.
