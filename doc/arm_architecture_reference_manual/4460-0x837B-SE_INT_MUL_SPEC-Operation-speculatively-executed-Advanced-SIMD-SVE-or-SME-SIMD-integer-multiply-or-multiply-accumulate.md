##### `0x837B`, SE\_INT\_MUL\_SPEC, Operation speculatively executed, Advanced SIMD, SVE, or SME SIMD integer multiply or multiply-accumulate

The counter counts each [speculatively executed](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-1-Definitions/-D14-1-1-Definition-of-terms?lang=en#cacijece) integer multiply, multiply-add, or multiply-subtract operation counted by [SE\_INT\_SPEC](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x8379--SE-INT-SPEC--Operation-speculatively-executed--Advanced-SIMD--SVE--or-SME-SIMD-integer--data-processing?lang=en#event_se_int_spec) due to an A64 Advanced SIMD, SVE, or SME operation.

That is, each operation counted by [ASE\_INT\_MUL\_SPEC](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x8049--ASE-INT-MUL-SPEC--Integer-operation-speculatively-executed--Advanced-SIMD-multiply?lang=en#event_ase_int_mul_spec), [SVE\_INT\_MUL\_SPEC](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x804A--SVE-INT-MUL-SPEC--Integer-operation-speculatively-executed--SVE-multiply?lang=en#event_sve_int_mul_spec), or [SME\_INT\_MUL\_SPEC](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x837A--SME-INT-MUL-SPEC--Operation-speculatively-executed--SME-integer-multiply-or-multiply-accumulate?lang=en#event_sme_int_mul_spec).
