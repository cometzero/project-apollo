# ​D21.9.1 Behavior when a PARTID is out-of-range

##### D21.9.1 Behavior when a PARTID is out-of-range

R YGRQN
:   When a physical or virtual [PARTID](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D21-MPAM-PE-Architecture/-D21-3-Partition-Identifiers--PARTIDs-?lang=en#mpam_partition_identifiers) from an [MPAMn\_ELx](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/-Appendix-K14-Registers-Index/-K14-1-Introduction-and-register-disambiguation/-K14-1-2-Register-name-disambiguation-by-Exception-level?lang=en#mpamn_elx).PARTID\_D or [MPAMn\_ELx](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/-Appendix-K14-Registers-Index/-K14-1-Introduction-and-register-disambiguation/-K14-1-2-Register-name-disambiguation-by-Exception-level?lang=en#mpamn_elx).PARTID\_I field is *out-of-range*, the behavior is a CONSTRAINED UNPREDICTABLE choice of:

    - The out-of-range PARTID is replaced by any in-range PARTID.
    - The out-of-range PARTID is replaced by the [Default physical PARTID](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D21-MPAM-PE-Architecture/-D21-3-Partition-Identifiers--PARTIDs-/-D21-3-2-The-Default-PARTID?lang=en#mpam_default_partid).

    For the replacement PARTID space of a replacement PARTID, see [PARTID space on error](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D21-MPAM-PE-Architecture/-D21-9-MPAM-errors/-D21-9-5-PARTID-space-on-error?lang=en#mpam_partid_space_on_error).
