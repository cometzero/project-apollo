##### Part A, Introduction and Architecture Overview

Part A gives an overview of the Armv8-A and Armv9-A architecture profiles, including its relationship to the other Arm PE architectures. It introduces the terminology used to describe the architecture, and gives an overview of the Executions states, AArch64 and AArch32.
