##### `0x82D0`, L1I\_LFB\_HIT\_RETIRED, Instruction architecturally executed, line-fill buffer hit in Level 1 instruction cache

The counter counts each [instruction architecturally executed](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-1-Definitions/-D14-1-1-Definition-of-terms?lang=en#cacciiig) counted by [L1I\_CACHE\_HIT\_RETIRED](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x82E0--L1I-CACHE-HIT-RETIRED--Instruction-architecturally-executed--hit-in-Level-1-instruction-cache?lang=en#event_l1i_cache_hit_retired) that hit a recently fetched line in Level 1 instruction or unified cache when fetched.
