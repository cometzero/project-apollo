##### `0x82E0`, L1I\_CACHE\_HIT\_RETIRED, Instruction architecturally executed, hit in Level 1 instruction cache

The counter counts each [instruction architecturally executed](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-1-Definitions/-D14-1-1-Definition-of-terms?lang=en#cacciiig) counted by [INST\_RETIRED](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x0008--INST-RETIRED--Instruction-architecturally-executed?lang=en#event_inst_retired) that hit in Level 1 instruction or unified cache when fetched.
