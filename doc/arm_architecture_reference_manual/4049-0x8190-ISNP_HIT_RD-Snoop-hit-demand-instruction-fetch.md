Source: <https://developer.arm.com/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x8190--ISNP-HIT-RD--Snoop-hit--demand-instruction-fetch>

##### `0x8190`, ISNP\_HIT\_RD, Snoop hit, demand instruction fetch

The counter counts each snoop generated by the PE in response to a demand [Instruction memory access](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-1-Definitions/-D14-1-1-Definition-of-terms?lang=en#cachjjbe) that hits in a cache outside of the cache hierarchy of this PE.

The event is counted by the PE generating the snoop, not the PE being snooped.
