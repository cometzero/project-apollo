##### `0x8292`, ISNP\_HIT\_FAR\_PRF, Snoop hit in far local cache, instruction prefetch

The counter counts each snoop generated by the PE in response to an instruction prefetch instruction, or by an instruction hardware prefetch counted by [ISNP\_HIT\_PRF](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x8290--ISNP-HIT-PRF--Snoop-hit--instruction-prefetch?lang=en#event_isnp_hit_prf) that hits in a cache outside the local PE cluster on the same device.

The event is counted by the PE generating the snoop, not the PE being snooped.
