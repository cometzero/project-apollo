##### I4.1.3 Access during reset and power transitions

As described in [Power and reset domains](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D16-The-Activity-Monitors-Extension/-D16-2-Properties-and-behavior-of-the-activity-monitors/-D16-2-3-Power-and-reset-domains?lang=en#cacifdhg), the power and reset domains of the Activity Monitoring Unit are named the AMU domain and AMU reset, and when reset of the AMU power domain occurs, the Activity Monitoring Unit is reset and the counters are reset to zero.

If the AMU domain is an always-on power domain, while the PE is reset or powered down counter values may be preserved and might be accessible by memory-mapped access.

If the AMU domain is the Core power domain, while the PE is reset or powered down and when a memory-mapped access occurs, the access reads as zero and the bus access completes without an error.
