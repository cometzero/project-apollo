##### F2.4.3 Multiply instructions

These instructions can operate on signed or unsigned quantities. In some types of operation, the results are the same whether the operands are signed or unsigned.

- [Table F2-4](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-F-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets/-Chapter-F2-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets-Overview/-F2-4-Data-processing-instructions/-F2-4-3-Multiply-instructions?lang=en#tbl_babhgeia) summarizes the multiply instructions where there is no distinction between signed and unsigned quantities.

  The least significant 32 bits of the result are used. More significant bits are discarded.
- [Table F2-5](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-F-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets/-Chapter-F2-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets-Overview/-F2-4-Data-processing-instructions/-F2-4-3-Multiply-instructions?lang=en#tbl_babibaed) summarizes the signed multiply instructions.
- [Table F2-6](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-F-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets/-Chapter-F2-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets-Overview/-F2-4-Data-processing-instructions/-F2-4-3-Multiply-instructions?lang=en#tbl_babfbgec) summarizes the unsigned multiply instructions.
