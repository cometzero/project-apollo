##### `0x8339`, L1GCS\_CACHE\_REFILL\_HWPRF, Level 1 GCS cache refill, hardware prefetch

The counter counts each hardware prefetch counted by [L1GCS\_CACHE\_HWPRF](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x8332--L1GCS-CACHE-HWPRF--Level-1-GCS-cache-hardware-prefetch?lang=en#event_l1gcs_cache_hwprf) that causes a refill of a Level 1 cache from outside of the Level 1 cache.
