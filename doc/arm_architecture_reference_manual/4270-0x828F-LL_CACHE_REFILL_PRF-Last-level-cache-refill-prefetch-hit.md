##### `0x828F`, LL\_CACHE\_REFILL\_PRF, Last level cache refill, prefetch hit

The counter counts each access counted by [LL\_CACHE\_PRF](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x8287--LL-CACHE-PRF--Last-level-cache--prefetch-hit?lang=en#event_ll_cache_prf) that causes a refill of the Last level cache, or any other data, instruction, or unified cache of this PE, from outside of those caches.
