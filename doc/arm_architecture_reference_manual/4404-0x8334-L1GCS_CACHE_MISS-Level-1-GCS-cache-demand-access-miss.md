##### `0x8334`, L1GCS\_CACHE\_MISS, Level 1 GCS cache demand access miss

The counter counts each access counted by [L1GCS\_CACHE\_RW](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x8331--L1GCS-CACHE-RW--Level-1-GCS-cache-demand-access?lang=en#event_l1gcs_cache_rw) that misses in the Level 1 caches causing an access to outside of the Level 1 caches of this PE.
