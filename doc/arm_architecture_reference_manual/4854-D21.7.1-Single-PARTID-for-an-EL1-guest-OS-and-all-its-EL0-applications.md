##### D21.7.1 Single PARTID for an EL1 guest OS and all its EL0 applications

R FNKGZ
:   Setting [MPAMHCR\_EL2](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-12-MPAMHCR-EL2--MPAM-Hypervisor-Control-Register--EL2-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpamhcr_el2).GSTAPP\_PLK to 1 causes [MPAM1\_EL1](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-2-MPAM1-EL1--MPAM1-Register--EL1-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam1_el1) to supply [PARTIDs](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D21-MPAM-PE-Architecture/-D21-3-Partition-Identifiers--PARTIDs-?lang=en#mpam_partition_identifiers) and [PMGs](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D21-MPAM-PE-Architecture/-D21-4-Performance-Monitoring-Groups--PMGs-?lang=en#mpam_pmg) for both EL1 and EL0, and causes [MPAM0\_EL1](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-1-MPAM0-EL1--MPAM0-Register--EL1-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam0_el1) to be unused, meaning that an EL1 guest OS and all its EL0 applications are in the same partition.

:   When [MPAMHCR\_EL2](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-12-MPAMHCR-EL2--MPAM-Hypervisor-Control-Register--EL2-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpamhcr_el2).{GSTAPP\_PLK, EL1\_VPMEN} is {1, 1}, the PARTID that results from the mapping process is the PARTID that the MPAM PE Requester sends for requests originating from EL0, as well as for those originating from EL1.
