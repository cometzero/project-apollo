##### F3.1.13 Floating-point data-processing

This section describes the encoding of the Floating-point data-processing group. This group has encodings in both the T32 and A32 instruction sets. For information about mappings between the encodings of this group, see [About the T32 Advanced SIMD and floating-point instructions and their encoding](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-F-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets/-Chapter-F3-T32-Instruction-Set-Encoding/-F3-2-About-the-T32-Advanced-SIMD-and-floating-point-instructions-and-their-encoding?lang=en#t32_encoding_index_mappings)
