##### `0x828E`, L3D\_CACHE\_REFILL\_PRF, Level 3 data cache refill, prefetch hit

The counter counts each access counted by [L3D\_CACHE\_PRF](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x8286--L3D-CACHE-PRF--Level-3-data-cache--prefetch-hit?lang=en#event_l3d_cache_prf) that causes a refill of the Level 3 data or unified cache, or any Level 1 or Level 2 data, instruction, or unified cache of this PE, from outside of those caches.
