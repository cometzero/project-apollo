# ​G4.7.1 EL2 control of the maintenance scope of Data Synchronization Barrier instructions executed at EL0 or EL1

##### G4.7.1 EL2 control of the maintenance scope of Data Synchronization Barrier instructions executed at EL0 or EL1

In an implementation that includes EL2 and that supports maintenance scope limitations on the [`DSB`](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-F-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets/-Chapter-F5-T32-and-A32-Base-Instruction-Set-Instruction-Descriptions/-F5-1-Alphabetical-list-of-T32-and-A32-base-instruction-set-instructions/-F5-1-48-DSB?lang=en#isa_dsb_a32) instructions, the [HCR](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-G-The-AArch32-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-G8-AArch32-System-Register-Descriptions/-G8-2-General-system-control-registers/-G8-2-65-HCR--Hyp-Configuration-Register?lang=en#reg_aarch32_hcr).BSU field can modify the required maintenance scope of an instruction that is executed at EL0 or EL1 in Non-secure state.
