- *OCP 8-bit Floating Point Specification (OFP8)* Paulius Micikevicius, Stuart Oberman, Pradeep Dubey, Marius Cornea, Andres Rodriguez, Ian Bratt, Richard Grisenthwaite, et al., June 2023.
