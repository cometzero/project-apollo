This section describes the encoding of the Floating-point data-processing group. The encodings in this section are decoded from [System register access, Advanced SIMD, floating-point, and Supervisor call](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-F-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets/-Chapter-F4-A32-Instruction-Set-Encoding/-F4-1-A32-instruction-set-encoding/-F4-1-12-System-register-access--Advanced-SIMD--floating-point--and-Supervisor-call?lang=en#a32_encoding_index_cops_as).

This group has encodings in both the T32 and A32 instruction sets. For information about mappings between the encodings of this group, see [About the A32 Advanced SIMD and floating-point instructions and their encoding](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-F-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets/-Chapter-F4-A32-Instruction-Set-Encoding/-F4-2-About-the-A32-Advanced-SIMD-and-floating-point-instructions-and-their-encoding?lang=en#a32_encoding_index_mappings)
