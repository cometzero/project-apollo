##### `0x8200`, L1I\_CACHE\_HIT, Level 1 instruction cache hit

The counter counts each access counted by [L1I\_CACHE](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x0014--L1I-CACHE--Level-1-instruction-cache-access?lang=en#event_l1i_cache) that hits in the Level 1 instruction or unified cache.
