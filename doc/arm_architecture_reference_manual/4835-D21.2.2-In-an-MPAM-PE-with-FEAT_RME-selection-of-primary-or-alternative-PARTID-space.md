R DFMYN
:   For execution below EL3, EL3 is either:

    - Forcing primary or alternative PARTID spaces for execution at EL2, EL1, and EL0.
    - Permitting EL2 to control its own PARTID space, and the PARTID space for EL1 and EL0.

    Forcing primary or alternative PARTID spaces for execution at EL2, EL1, and EL0, is by:

    1. [MPAM3\_EL3](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-4-MPAM3-EL3--MPAM3-Register--EL3-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam3_el3).ALTSP\_HEN=0 disabling the EL2 *alternative PARTID space controls*.
    2. [MPAM3\_EL3](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-4-MPAM3-EL3--MPAM3-Register--EL3-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam3_el3).ALTSP\_HFC selecting the primary or alternative PARTID space for execution at EL2, EL1, and EL0.

    Permitting EL2 to control its own PARTID space, and the PARTID space for EL1 and EL0, is by:

    - [MPAM3\_EL3](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-4-MPAM3-EL3--MPAM3-Register--EL3-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam3_el3).ALTSP\_HEN=1 enabling the EL2 alternative PARTID space controls, which means that:

      - EL2 can configure [MPAM2\_EL2](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-3-MPAM2-EL2--MPAM2-Register--EL2-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam2_el2).ALTSP\_EL2 to select the primary or alternative PARTID space for EL2.
      - EL2 can configure [MPAM2\_EL2](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-3-MPAM2-EL2--MPAM2-Register--EL2-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam2_el2).ALTSP\_HFC to select the primary or alternative PARTID space for EL1 and EL0.

    See the following tables for execution at EL2, and at EL1 and EL0, respectively:

R KXKGR
:   When EL2 is disabled in the current Security state, EL1 and EL0 produce the alternative PARTID space when [MPAM3\_EL3](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-4-MPAM3-EL3--MPAM3-Register--EL3-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam3_el3).{ALTSP\_HEN, ALTSP\_HFC} are {0, 1}, and produce the primary PARTID space otherwise.

R BTVYC
:   When [MPAM3\_EL3](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-4-MPAM3-EL3--MPAM3-Register--EL3-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam3_el3).MPAMEN is 0, so that MPAM is disabled from EL3, [MPAM2\_EL2](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-3-MPAM2-EL2--MPAM2-Register--EL2-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam2_el2).{ALTSP\_HFC, ALTSP\_EL2} are treated as {0, 0}, and [MPAM3\_EL3](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-4-MPAM3-EL3--MPAM3-Register--EL3-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam3_el3).{ALTSP\_HEN, ALTSP\_HFC, ALTSP\_EL3, RT\_ALTSP\_NS} are effective.

I HDJCV
:   [MPAMn\_ELx](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/-Appendix-K14-Registers-Index/-K14-1-Introduction-and-register-disambiguation/-K14-1-2-Register-name-disambiguation-by-Exception-level?lang=en#mpamn_elx).ALTSP\_FRCD reports whether execution at ELn is being forced to produce the alternative PARTID space:

    - [MPAM2\_EL2](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-3-MPAM2-EL2--MPAM2-Register--EL2-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam2_el2).ALTSP\_FRCD reports, in all implemented Security states, whether EL3 is forcing the alternative PARTID space for execution at EL2.
    - [MPAM1\_EL1](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-2-MPAM1-EL1--MPAM1-Register--EL1-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam1_el1).ALTSP\_FRCD reports, in all implemented Security states, whether EL3 or EL2 is forcing the alternative PARTID space for execution at EL1 and EL0.

    There is no ALTSP\_FRCD field in [MPAM0\_EL1](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-1-MPAM0-EL1--MPAM0-Register--EL1-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam0_el1). There is no mechanism for EL0 to discover whether it is producing the primary or alternative PARTID space. However, EL0 is always producing the same PARTID space as either:

    - EL2, when the [Effective value](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/Glossary?lang=en#effective_value) of [HCR\_EL2](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-2-General-system-control-registers/-D24-2-61-HCR-EL2--Hypervisor-Configuration-Register?lang=en#reg_aarch64_hcr_el2).{E2H, TGE} is {1, 1}.
    - EL1 otherwise.
