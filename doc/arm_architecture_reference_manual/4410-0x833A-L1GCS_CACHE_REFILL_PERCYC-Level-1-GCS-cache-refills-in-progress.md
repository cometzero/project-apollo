##### `0x833A`, L1GCS\_CACHE\_REFILL\_PERCYC, Level 1 GCS cache refills in progress

The counter increments by the number of Level 1 GCS cache refills counted by [L1GCS\_CACHE\_REFILL](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x8338--L1GCS-CACHE-REFILL--Level-1-GCS-cache-refill?lang=en#event_l1gcs_cache_refill) in progress on each [Processor cycle](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-1-Definitions/-D14-1-1-Definition-of-terms?lang=en#cacgcfhh).

The ratio [L1GCS\_CACHE\_REFILL\_PERCYC](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x833A--L1GCS-CACHE-REFILL-PERCYC--Level-1-GCS-cache-refills-in-progress?lang=en#event_l1gcs_cache_refill_percyc) ÷ [L1GCS\_CACHE\_REFILL](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x8338--L1GCS-CACHE-REFILL--Level-1-GCS-cache-refill?lang=en#event_l1gcs_cache_refill) is the mean duration of Level 1 GCS cache refills in [Processor cycles](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-1-Definitions/-D14-1-1-Definition-of-terms?lang=en#cacgcfhh).
