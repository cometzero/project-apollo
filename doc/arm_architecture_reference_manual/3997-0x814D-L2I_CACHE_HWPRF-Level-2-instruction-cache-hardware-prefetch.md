##### `0x814D`, L2I\_CACHE\_HWPRF, Level 2 instruction cache hardware prefetch

The counter counts each access counted by [L2I\_CACHE\_PRF](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x8281--L2I-CACHE-PRF--Level-2-instruction-cache--prefetch-hit?lang=en#event_l2i_cache_prf) that is due to a hardware prefetch.

The hardware prefetch is generated by a hardware prefetcher at the Level 2 instruction or unified cache.
