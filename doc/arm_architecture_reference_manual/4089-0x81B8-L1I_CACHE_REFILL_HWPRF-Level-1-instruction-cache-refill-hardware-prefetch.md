##### `0x81B8`, L1I\_CACHE\_REFILL\_HWPRF, Level 1 instruction cache refill, hardware prefetch

The counter counts each hardware prefetch counted by [L1I\_CACHE\_HWPRF](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x8145--L1I-CACHE-HWPRF--Level-1-instruction-cache-hardware-prefetch?lang=en#event_l1i_cache_hwprf) that causes a refill of the Level 1 instruction or unified cache from outside of the Level 1 instruction or unified cache.
