##### D7.5.13 Branch prediction

In the AArch64 architecture, branch predictors are not architecturally visible.

For details on the restrictions on the effects of speculation for branch predictors, see [Restrictions on the effects of speculation](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-B-The-AArch64-Application-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-B2-The-AArch64-Application-Level-Memory-Model/-B2-5-Restrictions-on-the-effects-of-speculation?lang=en#chdhjgda).
