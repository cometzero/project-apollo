##### `0x8201`, L2I\_CACHE\_HIT, Level 2 instruction cache hit

The counter counts each access counted by [L2I\_CACHE](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x0027--L2I-CACHE--Level-2-instruction-cache-access?lang=en#event_l2i_cache) that hits in the Level 2 instruction or unified cache.
