    - If EL3 is the highest implemented Exception level, [MPAM3\_EL3](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-4-MPAM3-EL3--MPAM3-Register--EL3-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam3_el3).MPAMEN is the writable MPAM enable control, and [MPAM2\_EL2](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-3-MPAM2-EL2--MPAM2-Register--EL2-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam2_el2).MPAMEN and [MPAM1\_EL1](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D24-AArch64-System-Register-Descriptions/-D24-12-MPAM-registers/-D24-12-2-MPAM1-EL1--MPAM1-Register--EL1-?lang=en#reg_aarch64_mpam1_el1).MPAMEN are read-only copies.
