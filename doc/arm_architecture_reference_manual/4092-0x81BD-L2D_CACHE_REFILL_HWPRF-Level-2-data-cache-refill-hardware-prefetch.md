##### `0x81BD`, L2D\_CACHE\_REFILL\_HWPRF, Level 2 data cache refill, hardware prefetch

The counter counts each hardware prefetch counted by [L2D\_CACHE\_HWPRF](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D14-PMU-Event-Descriptions/-D14-3-Common-event-numbers/-0x8155--L2D-CACHE-HWPRF--Level-2-data-cache-hardware-prefetch?lang=en#event_l2d_cache_hwprf) that causes a refill of the Level 2 data or unified cache, or any Level 1 data, instruction, or unified cache of this PE, from outside of those caches.
