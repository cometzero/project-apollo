##### D21.1.5 MPAMn\_ELx register naming convention

I FFCGH
:   In an MPAM PE, [MPAMn\_ELx](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/-Appendix-K14-Registers-Index/-K14-1-Introduction-and-register-disambiguation/-K14-1-2-Register-name-disambiguation-by-Exception-level?lang=en#mpamn_elx) registers contain the [PARTIDs](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D21-MPAM-PE-Architecture/-D21-3-Partition-Identifiers--PARTIDs-?lang=en#mpam_partition_identifiers) and [PMGs](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D21-MPAM-PE-Architecture/-D21-4-Performance-Monitoring-Groups--PMGs-?lang=en#mpam_pmg) that the MPAM PE sends as part of [MPAM information bundles](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-D-The-AArch64-System-Level-Architecture/-Chapter-D21-MPAM-PE-Architecture/-D21-1-About-MPAM/-D21-1-2-MPAM-information-bundles?lang=en#mpam_mpam_information_bundles). The naming convention of these registers is:

    - The n in [MPAMn\_ELx](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-K-Appendixes/-Appendix-K14-Registers-Index/-K14-1-Introduction-and-register-disambiguation/-K14-1-2-Register-name-disambiguation-by-Exception-level?lang=en#mpamn_elx) is the Exception level the register holds PARTIDs and PMGs for. That is, the register supplies PARTIDs and PMGs for execution at ELn.
    - The x means that ELx and higher can access the register.
