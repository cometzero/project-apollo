##### F6.1.158 VORN (immediate)

Vector Bitwise OR NOT (immediate) performs a bitwise OR between a register value and the complement of an immediate value, and returns the result into the destination vector.

This is a pseudo-instruction of [VORR (immediate)](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-F-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets/-Chapter-F6-T32-and-A32-Advanced-SIMD-and-Floating-point-Instruction-Descriptions/-F6-1-Alphabetical-list-of-Advanced-SIMD-and-floating-point-instructions/-F6-1-159-VORR--immediate-?lang=en#isa_vorr_i). This means:

- The encodings in this description are named to match the encodings of [VORR (immediate)](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-F-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets/-Chapter-F6-T32-and-A32-Advanced-SIMD-and-Floating-point-Instruction-Descriptions/-F6-1-Alphabetical-list-of-Advanced-SIMD-and-floating-point-instructions/-F6-1-159-VORR--immediate-?lang=en#isa_vorr_i).
- The assembler syntax is used only for assembly, and is not used on disassembly.
- The description of [VORR (immediate)](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-F-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets/-Chapter-F6-T32-and-A32-Advanced-SIMD-and-Floating-point-Instruction-Descriptions/-F6-1-Alphabetical-list-of-Advanced-SIMD-and-floating-point-instructions/-F6-1-159-VORR--immediate-?lang=en#isa_vorr_i) gives the operational pseudocode, any CONSTRAINED UNPREDICTABLE behavior, and any operational information for this instruction.

It has encodings from the following instruction sets: A32 ([A1](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-F-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets/-Chapter-F6-T32-and-A32-Advanced-SIMD-and-Floating-point-Instruction-Descriptions/-F6-1-Alphabetical-list-of-Advanced-SIMD-and-floating-point-instructions/-F6-1-158-VORN--immediate-?lang=en#isa_vorn_vorr_i_iclass_a1) and [A2](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-F-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets/-Chapter-F6-T32-and-A32-Advanced-SIMD-and-Floating-point-Instruction-Descriptions/-F6-1-Alphabetical-list-of-Advanced-SIMD-and-floating-point-instructions/-F6-1-158-VORN--immediate-?lang=en#isa_vorn_vorr_i_iclass_a2)) and T32 ([T1](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-F-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets/-Chapter-F6-T32-and-A32-Advanced-SIMD-and-Floating-point-Instruction-Descriptions/-F6-1-Alphabetical-list-of-Advanced-SIMD-and-floating-point-instructions/-F6-1-158-VORN--immediate-?lang=en#isa_vorn_vorr_i_iclass_t1) and [T2](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-F-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets/-Chapter-F6-T32-and-A32-Advanced-SIMD-and-Floating-point-Instruction-Descriptions/-F6-1-Alphabetical-list-of-Advanced-SIMD-and-floating-point-instructions/-F6-1-158-VORN--immediate-?lang=en#isa_vorn_vorr_i_iclass_t2)).

:   ​

    Is a constant of the specified type that is replicated to fill the destination register. For details of the range of constants available and the encoding of `<imm>`, see [Modified immediate constants in T32 and A32 Advanced SIMD instructions](/documentation/ddi0487/mc/-Part-F-The-AArch32-Instruction-Sets/-Chapter-F1-About-the-T32-and-A32-Instruction-Descriptions/-F1-7-General-information-about-the-T32-and-A32-instruction-descriptions/-F1-7-7-Modified-immediate-constants-in-T32-and-A32-instructions?lang=en#cjaidjdj).

<Qd>
:   ​

    Is the 128-bit name of the SIMD&FP destination register, encoded in the “D:Vd” field as <Qd>\*2.
